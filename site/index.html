Abstract

We answer Problem 30 in Section 9 (p. 27) of Alfuraidan, Khan and Sauras-Altuzarra, Metric dimension and maximum eigenvalue multiplicity of graph Laplacians, arXiv:2610.26875v1, and extend the metric-dimension classification of Knor, Sedlar and Škrekovski (2022) to a classification of both maximum spectral multiplicities for Theta(3,3,r), for every r >= 2. For every integer k >= 1, the theta graph with edge lengths 3, 3, 6k+4 is connected and twin-free, has metric dimension and maximum Laplacian eigenvalue multiplicity two, and has simple signless spectrum. Its only repeated Laplacian eigenvalues are 1 and 3, each double. Distinct orders give pairwise nonisomorphic examples. For r >= 3, repeated eigenvalues are classified modulo six; r = 2 has a separate quadratic repeated factor. Attaching an equal pendant path at every vertex of two known base graphs also gives twin-free infinite families in both separation directions.
